Who actually delivers

Claimed track record vs public receipts

When a company announces its GCC, it often names the firm that built it. Those announcements are receipts. Counting them gives a buyer something to check, instead of a number on a sales slide. The same rules apply to every provider, including Flexiple.

A low evidenced count does not mean a provider is weak. It means its work is not yet public, and the provider can fix that by pointing to client announcements. In production, providers would claim their profile and submit receipts, and each one would be graded the same way.

Method

How a fact gets into the ledger

The data mostly exists already. It is scattered and ungraded. The work is collecting it, keeping the disagreements, and showing a receipt for every number.

1 · Collect

Pull public signals

Company press releases, state policy notifications, MoUs with investment agencies, real-estate reports, MCA filings, and Flexiple's own talent graph.

2 · Extract

Read with an LLM

Each document becomes structured records: who, where, how big, which partner, with a short quote kept as proof.

3 · Reconcile

Match, don't overwrite

Records merge on the company. A second source that disagrees becomes a recorded conflict, never a silent replacement.

4 · Grade

Stamp A, B or C

A for the originator, B for a roundup restating it, C for a post with no traceable origin. Grades rise when a better source turns up.

5 · Publish

Every number, a receipt

Pages and the AI desk only show what has a source. Anything else is labelled as an assumption.

Grades

Neutrality rules

  • Flexiple sells GCC setup, so the ledger never ranks providers. It only counts receipts.
  • Flexiple is held to the same rules as every other provider and appears in the same table.
  • The method and every source are public. Anyone can dispute a record with a better receipt.
  • Lead routing is opt-in and comes after the plan, never inside it.

What is real in this prototype

  • All 63 sources were read for this build between 17 and 18 Oct 2026. Links open the originals.
  • Salary medians and verified counts are Flexiple's published numbers. Rents are CRE Matrix's.
  • The cost model's loaded multiplier and seat size are assumptions, marked wherever they are used.
  • Incentive checks simplify each policy to its headline tests. A real version would encode the notified rules clause by clause.
  • AI is optional. Without it, the desk searches the ledger, the memo is assembled from the figures and extraction uses rules. With AI on, Claude writes the same three outputs from the same data.
